Multimodal Writing

English 1301 — UTRGV

Target

My plan for this mutlimodel project is to be able to use the skills of web devlopment to be able to create a stunning way for my audience to engage in my writing and also learn more about what I'm interested in.

Approach

I decided to use the help of VSCode, HTML, and JavaScript to create this website. My initial steps were to create my theme around one of my favorite games of all time, Persona 5. The sharp red & black being used in the game to show rebellion, as the stars in the back having a black and white style. The reason I chose these to take out of the games style is that its often seen throughout the game and the plot of the whole story is that people must use their free will and not be stuck in chains of life. This meaning of the chains is displaying how the world is suppressed while people cannot seek true justice until the take of the mask.

What I Learned

I learned how to use different types of genres that help shaped the creation of my website programming in HTML for my second time I learned how to add music which creates a nice vibe when scrolling throughout the website and with the accent colors popping throughout. Overall it’s been a fun project and helped me to learn more of my personal skills.
